The industry, on court.
I've never played. Is this for me?

Yes. Our First Serve slot is designed for complete beginners, and every session is social first.

Can my company enter a team?

Yes. The corporate league is for company teams. Request an invite, mention it in your role, and we'll be in touch about captaining.

Is there a joining fee?

No. Membership is free. You only pay for the games you play, and Founding Members get their first four on us.

Who can join?

People across construction and FM, including cleaning, security, soft FM, property and workplace. Site or office, every level of player. Every member is approved personally, or nominated by someone already in the club.

Where do we play?

In East London, at Padium Canary Wharf and Stratford Padel Club.
